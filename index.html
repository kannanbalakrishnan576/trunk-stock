What goes in the trunk

“No heat.” Six parts can fix it, but the trunk fits three. The problem code is known at dispatch, and so is the list of parts that can fix it. This page forecasts which of them each job will need, and turns that into the three or five parts to carry.

Every number is synthetic, generated by scripts/generate_data.py. Forecasts are TimesFM 3.0 run locally, with temperature and install-base units at risk as known-future covariates.

Rolling, 26 weeks (11 May – 2 Nov 2026). Each week, every method sees only the weeks before it and picks next week's list. Score: share of part-needing jobs whose part was in the trunk.

Top 3 carried. Each point pools four weeks, so a week with eight jobs does not swing the line. Where a line seems to vanish, it is under another: the two methods picked the same parts.
